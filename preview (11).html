An evidence-focused perspective

A professional reputation needs context.

Visibility can introduce a professional. A specific business decision requires more detail: the role, the deliverables, relevant experience and the terms of the proposed relationship.

Build a useful business brief.

  1. State the objective. Describe the business problem and the improvement you want to assess.
  2. Define the engagement. Identify the contracting party, responsibilities, timetable and deliverables.
  3. Request comparable examples. Focus on work relevant to your industry and project requirements.
  4. Agree on evaluation. Establish what success means and how both parties will assess it.

These are general engagement-planning principles, not a claim about Freihofer's documented methodology.
